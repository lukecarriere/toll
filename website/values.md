# Values

- Tax the action, not the identity. We do not score visitors as human or not.
- Reads are free unless an owner overrides that on purpose, and we warn them first.
- No puzzles. No tracking pixels. No selling visitor data.
- A person should not have to learn a new tool to post a comment.
- High-volume access can be paid per request. The owner sees dollars, not a protocol.
- We fail closed on writes and fail open on public pages.
- We do not block crawlers. Other tools do that. We do not pretend to be them.
